## What this Course is About

In this course you will learn to:

1.  Incorporate AI into apps you write

   - Use classical machine learning for things like:
     - Spam detection
     - Image recognition
     - Customer recommendations

   - Use generative AI for things like:
     - An AI game opponent
     - Answering customer questions

2. Write apps using AI

   - Use an AI coding assistant in an IDE

   - Use a full AI coding app like Claude Code

This course is part of the AI Programming CPC (Career Pathway Certificate) which consists of four courses:

- Two programming courses in any language: CS 161/133, 162/233 (python preferred).
- AI Programming 1 and 2: CS 212, CS 213

## Will AI Replace Software Developers?

- AI can write code fast and do it pretty well. Software developers will be expected to know how to use AI to write apps and how to incorporate AI into their apps.
- The role of the software developer is changing.
  - Writing software specifications
  - Setting up AI systems
  - Verifying software produced by AI
- There are currently less entry level jobs but there are predictions that they will come back when CEOs discover they don't have any new devs in the pipeline.

## Environmental, Social and Ethical Issues

### The Problems

- Environmental: AI data centers:

  -  Electrical power use.
  -  Water consumption.
  - Noise pollution.

- Social issues

  - Job loss
  - Dependecy on AI and loss of critical thinking and crativity
  - Mental health: AI fueling delusional thought or AI as a therapist
  - Isolation: using AI as a friend or for entertainment
  - Loss of humanity when AI takes over human roles like creativitiy and relationship.
  - AI used in policing and warfare.

- Ethical Issues

  - AI trained on the work of artists and authors without permission or compensation.
  - AI produced documents and media being published without being identified as being generated by AI.
  - Fake AI images and videos imitating real people.
  - AI scams and cybercrime.

  ### Potential Mitigations

- Data Centers

  - Alternate power sources like solar, wind and nuclear.
  - Closed loop cooling.
  - Underground, underwater and space based data centers.
  - Use small models that run on a local PC
  - Use medium sized models that run on a workstation.

- Job loss&mdash;especially for software devs

  - There might be a "backlash" when too many senior devs retire with no one to replace them. Or when too much bad software was written by unsupervised AI.
  - The Software dev job role will change:
    - Focus more on systems analysis, systems design and verification.
    - Morph into AI systems designer and manager (a new kind of dev ops?).
  - More software will be needed to do more things that are now possible with AI and to combat the bad things AI does.

- Social and ethical issues

  - As a dev:
    - Don't train models on other people's IP.
    - Don't write apps that cause harm.
  - Government regulation.
  - Education regardinng the dangers of AI.
  - Making choices about what AI providers to support.
